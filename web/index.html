The Steiner-Parker filter of Sofia Vallejo (IEM, KUG Graz): a nonlinear wave digital model of the Yusynth VCF, Vult build. Here written in Faust (dsp/steinerparker.lib), compiled to WebAssembly in double precision by faust-rs, with the Newton loop that exits as the original's does. Its output matches the original C++ to about -120 dB (results). The source is a test signal at 1.0 = 5 V; the filter runs at the page's sample rate. Quality switches between three compiled programs, and restarts the state at the DC operating point, as the plugin does.
